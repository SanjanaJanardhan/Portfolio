About me

A little about me.

I'm Sanjana Janardhan, just graduated with an M.Eng. in Computer Science at Cornell University. I did my undergrad in CS too; turns out one degree wasn't quite enough to scratch the itch. I like understanding how things actually work, not just that they do.

I've worked across the stack: building backend systems, wiring up cloud infrastructure, and dabbling in AI when the problem calls for it. At Maersk, I got to see what it looks like when software runs at real scale. That stuck with me, and it's shaped how I think about building things.

When I'm not at a keyboard, I'm probably singing, or hunting down a restaurant I've never tried, or going down a rabbit hole on something that has nothing to do with my to-do list. I like people, I like good food, and I genuinely believe the best ideas come from having interests outside your field.
